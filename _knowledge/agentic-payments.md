## The idea

Agentic payments refer to AI agents being able to initiate and complete
transactions on a person's behalf — booking, buying, subscribing — without a
human clicking "confirm" at every step.

## Why it's interesting

- It shifts trust from "human approves each purchase" to "human sets rules,
  agent operates within them."
- Raises real questions around authentication, spending limits, and how an
  agent proves it's acting on behalf of a specific person.

## Open questions I still have

- How do merchants verify an agent is authorized, versus a compromised one?
- What does a "receipt" or audit trail look like when the buyer is software?
